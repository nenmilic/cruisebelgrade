I was born in Belgrade and I have worked in its restaurants, which mostly
means I know which kitchens cut corners and which ones don't. We skip the
places that live off passing tourist traffic and go where I eat.

The staff know me at every stop, so there's a table waiting and usually
something that never made it onto the menu.

Vegetarians are fine here — tell me when you book and I'll arrange it ahead
rather than putting you on the spot at the table.
