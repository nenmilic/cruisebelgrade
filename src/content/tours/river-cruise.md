The shortest of the three trips, and the one to take if you have an afternoon rather than a day. No restaurant stop, no long lunch, just the rivers, the bridges and the fortress from the water.

Best late in the day. The light drops behind the fortress and the whole waterfront turns colour, which is when Belgrade earns its reputation.

Six guests at most, and the boat is yours for the trip. I am at the tiller the whole way, so ask whatever you like.
